# Governance

Verbsmith is currently maintained by Sreejith Muralidharan. Decisions are made
in public issues and architecture decision records. Compatibility, privacy, and
data safety changes require a documented proposal before implementation.

Contributors who make sustained, constructive contributions may be invited as
maintainers. Maintainers must use two-factor authentication, disclose relevant
conflicts of interest, and recuse themselves where appropriate. Releases require
review from a maintainer other than the author once the project has two active
maintainers.

The project will not sell placement in documentation, manipulate popularity
metrics, or hide security-relevant limitations.
